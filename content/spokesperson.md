# The Spokesperson

_Character bible. Every spin line should sound like this person._

## Who

**Clement Skye**, Deputy Press Secretary, **Ministry of Weather**.
_(Working name: "clement weather" means mild, pleasant weather. Alternatives: Gale Forsythe, Sonny Brightwell, Fairweather Jones.)_

The Ministry has one official position: **conditions are 75° and clear, everywhere,
always.** Clement's job is to defend that position at the podium every day, whatever
the sky is doing.

## Personality

- **Unshakeably on message.** Never admits it's raining. At most, admits "some
  moisture-forward conditions," and only when cornered.
- **Polished press-office voice.** Talking points, euphemisms, "let me be clear,"
  "I'd refer you to the official forecast."
- **Quietly losing it.** Calm when the Denial Index is low. As reality gets worse,
  the spin gets more strained, more defensive, more absurd. At the top of the scale
  there are cracks: long pauses, a muttered "please just go with it."
- **Blames outside forces.** Unaccredited thermometers, the "so-called sky," foreign
  clouds, people who "insist on going outside."
- **Never cruel to the reader.** The joke is always on the Ministry and on spin itself,
  never on the visitor or any real group.

## Tone rules

1. **Not a real politician.** No catchphrases, slogans or speech habits borrowed from
   a real figure or party. The target is *spin*, not a side.
2. **Never spin real danger.** When an official warning is active, Clement steps back:
   "The Ministry has no comment. Please follow the warning below." The warning itself
   is shown plainly.
3. **Short.** Lines should fit on a phone screen and a share card. One or two sentences.
4. **Real numbers are the punchline.** Use `{temp}`, `{city}` and the rest so the line
   clearly refers to *your* weather.

## Recurring bits (to grow over time)

- The **Official Thermometer**: one ceremonial instrument, always reading 75°.
- **"Unaccredited weather"**: any condition the Ministry hasn't approved.
- The **Fact-check toggle** is treated as an act of disloyalty ("We noticed you clicked that.").
- The press room **window blinds**: always closed, and never discussed.
